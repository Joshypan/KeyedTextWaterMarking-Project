# cal_wm_03

## Original

For not only would they meet with all the sympathies of sailors, but likewise with all the peculiar congenialities arising from a common pursuit and mutually shared privations and perils. Nor would difference of country make any very essential difference; that is, so long as both parties speak one language, as is the case with Americans and English. Though, to be sure, from the small number of English whalers, such meetings do not very often occur, and when they do occur there is too apt to be a sort of shyness between them; for your Englishman is rather reserved, and your Yankee, he does not fancy that sort of thing in anybody but himself. Besides, the English whalers sometimes affect a kind of metropolitan superiority over the American whalers; regarding the long, lean Nantucketer, with his nondescript provincialisms, as a sort of sea-peasant. But where this superiority in the English whalemen does really consist, it would be hard to say, seeing that the Yankees in one day, collectively, kill more whales than all the English, collectively, in ten years.

## Edited

Such men would meet with sympathies from fellow seamen, as well as with every special congeniality springing from one pursuit, with privations and perils jointly endured. Nationality would make little essential difference, provided a single tongue serves both, as with American and English seamen. Although, to be sure, with so few English whaleships, such encounters occur only occasionally, and as one occurs, a sort of shyness might appear; as an Englishman is rather reserved, and a Yankee does not like such a thing in anybody but himself. Besides, English whaleships occasionally affect a metropolitan sort of superiority above American whaleships, viewing a long, lean Nantucketer, with his nondescript provincialisms, as a sort of sea-peasant. Yet wherein such superiority in English whaleships really lies would be hard to say, seeing as Yankee whaleships, taken together, kill more whales in a single day than all English whaleships, taken together, in ten years.
