With some readers, a charming freshness and wit in_ Northanger Abbey, _its wholeness, polish, and_ entrain, _hide certain critical facts: its scale is small; its scheme, after all, is burlesque or parody, a sort in which a first place is reached only with hard labour._ Persuasion, _comparatively mild in tone, and hardly gripping in interest, has admirers who prize its exquisite delicacy and keeping above every other novel.
