Perjury, oppression, subornation, fraud, pandarism, as well as similar failings, were amid the most pardonable arts admitted; and as was reasonable, I made ample allowance for these. Yet when some confessed owing greatness as well as riches to sodomy, or incest; others, to prostituting spouses as well as daughters; others, to betrayal of nation or monarch; some, to poison; more, to perverting justice so as to ruin blameless men, I trust I might be forgiven, if such findings made me diminish a little such deep reverence as I naturally have for men in lofty station, who ought to be treated by us inferiors with every respect owing to so sublime a dignity. I had read many times about eminent services done unto princes as well as states, and asked to see those who accomplished such services. Upon asking, I was informed “that such names would be found nowhere on record, besides a few, depicted in history as basest of villains and traitors. Concerning others, I had not once heard about them.
