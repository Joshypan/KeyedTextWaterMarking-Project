Gulliver, having grown fatigued by crowds of curious visitors who came to see him in his house in Redriff, bought a small plot of land, with a convenient house, near Newark, in Nottinghamshire, his native county; where he now lives in seclusion, yet is respected by neighbours. Although Gulliver was born in Nottinghamshire, where his father lived, yet I have heard him tell how his family came from Oxfordshire; which I can confirm, as I have observed in a churchyard in Banbury, in Oxfordshire, several tombs as well as monuments bearing the Gulliver name. Before he quitted Redriff, he left papers which follow in my care, with freedom to dispose of them as I might think proper. I have thrice perused them with care. Its style is quite clear as well as artless; my only fault to find is how its author, like many travellers, is a little too circumstantial.
