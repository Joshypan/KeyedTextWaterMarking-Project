Can you know every secret of life as well as death? Can you know comparative anatomy in full, so as to explain why something brutish is in certain men, yet absent in others? Can you tell why, as other spiders perish small and early, one giant spider lived for ages in a steeple of an old Spanish church, and grew and grew, till, upon descending, it might drink oil from every lamp in its church? Can you tell why in Pampas, as well as elsewhere, bats come in darkness, opening veins in cattle and horses, sucking them dry; how upon certain western islands bats cling on branches by day, which those who have seen them liken unto giant fruit, and how, as sailors lie asleep upon a ship in heat, they descend upon them, and then--and then in morning are found dead men, as pale as even Miss Lucy was?”
