It is obvious you are like your father. One can easily see when a man has a father whom heavens have blessed as regards wife as well as offspring—and heavens have blessed Nestor from first to last throughout his life, with a green old age at home, with sons about him who are well minded as well as courageous. So let us stop such weeping, and attend once more to supper. Let water be poured over our hands. Telemachus and I can talk fully with one another tomorrow morning.” Upon such words Asphalion, a servant, poured water on hands of every guest, and all laid hands upon food which was set before them. Then Helen, daughter of Jove, bethought her of a new matter.
