As an example, in a case like one above; judges have no wish to know which title my opponent has to my cow; but whether such a beast is black or red; whether her horns are long or short; what shape a meadow is in which she grazes, round or square; whether she was milked at home or elsewhere; which illnesses she is subject unto, with similar matters; after which they review precedents, adjourn a cause from time to time, and in ten, twenty, thirty years, come to an issue. “Observe likewise that such a body has a strange tongue, a cant quite its own, which no other human can comprehend, wherein all their laws are written, which they take special care to increase; so they have wholly confused truth with falsehood, right with wrong; so thirty years would be needed to settle whether a meadow handed down by my ancestors over six generations is mine, or a stranger's three hundred miles away.
