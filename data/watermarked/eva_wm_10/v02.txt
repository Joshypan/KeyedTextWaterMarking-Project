So all came home to native beaches, and heard old seals, their fathers, clashing in a drifting mist. That night Kotick danced the Fire-dance with yearling seals. The ocean is full of fire on summer nights, from Novastoshnah as far as Lukannon, and every seal has a wake like blazing oil, with a flaming flash as he jumps, as waves break in great phosphorescent streaks, swirling. Then every one headed inland to holluschickie grounds, rolled about in new wild wheat, and told tales about doings out on ocean waters. They talked about Pacific waters as boys would talk about a forest in which they had been nutting, and a listener able to follow them might have gone away and made a chart of that ocean finer than any ever made.
