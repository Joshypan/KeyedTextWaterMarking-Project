“Would it not be well to hear what I have to say? said Van Helsing. “And then you will at least know the limit of my purpose. Shall I go on?” “Miss Lucy is dead; is it not so? Yes! Then there can be no wrong to her. But if she be not dead----” “Good God! he cried. “What do you mean? Has there been any mistake; has she been buried alive? He groaned in anguish that not even hope could soften. “I did not say she was alive, my child; I did not think it. I go no further than to say that she might be Un-Dead.” “Un-Dead! Not alive! What do you mean? Is this all a nightmare, or what is it?” “There are mysteries which men can only guess at, which age by age they may solve only in part.
