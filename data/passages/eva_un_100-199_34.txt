He is such a man-cub as never was,” said Baloo. “The best and wisest and boldest of man-cubs--my own pupil, who shall make the name of Baloo famous through all the jungles; and besides, I--we--love him, Kaa.” “Ts! Ts! said Kaa, weaving his head to and fro. “I also have known what love is.
